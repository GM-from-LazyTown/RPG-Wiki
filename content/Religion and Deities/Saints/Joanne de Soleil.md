Half [[Human|human]], half [[Angel|angel]]. [[Saint]] and [[Priest|priestess]] of the [[Amariel - The Sun-Mother|Amariel]]. [[Adventurer]] and the leader of the [[Shining Spear]], known for freeing her people from the tyranny of vampire [[Lucien de Noirveil]] and establishing the [[Kingdom of Solaya]]. Wielder of the legendary artifact - [[Spear of the Sun]].
# Also known as
- Saint Joanne
- Hero of the [[Spear of the Sun]]

# Worship
The most commonly worshipped deity/saint in the [[Kingdom of Solaya]]. Almost every good and lawful citizen worship her in some manner.
