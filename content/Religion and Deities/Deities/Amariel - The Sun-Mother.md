**[[Amariel - The Sun-Mother]]** is the most commonly worshipped [[Deity|deity]] on the [[Continent of Berythos]]. Her churches are spread across the continent, with the most prominent ones located in the [[Papacy of Larangis]].

# Also known as
- The Good Mother
- The Blazing Sun

# Personal interventions
She is known to intervene rarely in human conflicts, mostly acting through her [[Priest|priests]] by performing [[Miracle|miracles]]. These typically include healing the wounded, creating protective wards, and smiting evil creatures.

# Worship
Belief in Amariel is strongest in the [[Kingdom of Solaya]], founded by her [[Saint|saint]], [[Joanne de Soleil]], who is believed to be her daughter.

Her churches and major organizations are concentrated in the [[Papacy of Larangis]], which serves as the central hub of her worship on the continent.
