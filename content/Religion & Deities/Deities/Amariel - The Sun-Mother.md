**[[Amariel - The Sun-Mother]]** is the most commonly worshipped [[Deity]] on the [[Continent of Berythos]]. She has churches spread over the whole continent, with the most prominent ones being located in the [[Papacy of Larangis]].

# Personal interventions
She's known to rarely intervene in human conflicts, mostly acting by performing [[Miracle|miracles]] though her [[Priests|priests]]. These consist mostly of healing the wounded, creating protective wards and smiting evil creatures.

# Worship
She's the most commonly worshipped [[Deity]] on the [[Continent of Berythos]]. She has churches spread over the whole continent, with the most important organizations and places of worship being located in the [[Papacy of Larangis]].

The country, where belief in her is the strongest is [[Kingdom of Solaya]], which has been established by [[Saint Joanne de Soleil]], who's being believed to be her own daughter.
