# Geography
Medium sized country in the south-west of the [[Continent of Berythos|Berythos]]. Surrounded by the [[TODO]] mountain range to the north and the [[TODO]] sea to the west and [[TODO]] sea to the south.

# History
The terrain of the kingdom used to be ruled by the [[Vampire]] Lord [[Lucien de Noirveil]], until the heroes of the [[Shining Spear]] has defeated him and established the kingdom, with their leader [[Joanne de Soleil]] as the first Queen.

# Cities
- [[Aurevia]] - Capital and the second biggest city in the kingdom. Home to the king and the most important organizations in the country
- [[Kereval]] - Old capital, from the time before the [[Shining Spear]]. Third biggest city in the country.
- [[Port-Emeraude]] - Biggest city in the kingdom, known as most important port, full of merchants.
- [[Dolbrac]] - Old capital of the recently subjugated country of [[Berfen]].
- [[Sombreval]] - Known as City of Blood. Ruled by [[Vampire|vampires]] allied to the kingdom.
- [[Troncay]] - City built next to the biggest forest of the kingdom, known for it's hot springs.
- [[Montferraud]] - Giant fortress, which is the first line of defence against the [[Empire of Alexios]].
