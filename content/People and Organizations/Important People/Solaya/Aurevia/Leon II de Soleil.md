# Biography / Overview
Leon II de Soleil is the reigning monarch of Solaya. He is a descendant of [[Joanne de Soleil]] and is loved and respected by his people for his wisdom and merciful nature. Leon has always had a weak constitution and is currently bedridden, suffering from an unknown illness.
